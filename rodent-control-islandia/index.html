Reading the calendar the way a rodent does.

Wind-driven storms can damage soffits, vents and trim, which opens routes into the building. After a bad night outside, walk the exterior and look for anything knocked loose.

The change of seasons is the easiest time for gaps to open. Wood shrinks and swells, caulk cracks and mortar loosens. A short check of vents, door seals and pipe collars in autumn can prevent a winter surprise.

Health agency advice, boiled down

Most of the job is avoiding airborne dust, and the steps beneath show how.

  • Dampen anything brittle before it is moved at all.
  • Leave a shut-up room thirty minutes with windows wide, and do not hover at the doorway.
  • Leave gloves on for the whole job, remove them carefully, rinse reusable ones and wash your hands.
  • Coat the waste and the floor around it, give it a rest, and only then make the first pass with a towel.
  • Tie the bag, put it in the bin, then give your hands another wash.
  • If a fever lingers or breathing becomes short after exposure, see your doctor.
