Easy steps to take until help arrives

A measured pace keeps fine particles from reaching your lungs.

  • Wiping with a dry rag raises the same particles that health agencies caution about.
  • CDC guidance calls for roughly thirty minutes of ventilation in a closed space before any cleaning.
  • Gloves should be on from the start, since dirty hands can carry waste to your mouth; soap and water afterward.
  • Coat the waste and the floor around it, give it a rest, and only then make the first pass with a towel.
  • The soiled towels may go in with the dead animal inside one tied bag, and a second bag goes over anything thin.
  • Feeling sick after contact with droppings is for a physician to judge, so tell them what you touched.
