The start of a booking.

A dispatcher picks up at (631) 869-8953 and puts a handful of questions to you. Where is the structure? What have you noticed, and in which spot? Roughly when did it start? Are young children or animals in the house? What you answer shapes who is sent and what loads onto the truck.

Calls at midnight, on Sundays and during holidays still reach a human being. Should no technician be available today, you are told right then, with a time window to plan around.

What a visit consists of.

  • A tour beginning at the roof line and moving through attic, cellar, garage and yard, looking for current signs.
  • Traps laid on the runs revealed by grease marks and pellets.
  • Openings stopped with flashing, woven steel and a hardening compound.
  • A second trip to tally catches, test each repair and schedule cleanup where it is warranted.

Order matters here. Sealing prematurely can leave an animal stuck inside the wall, and cleaning an attic while its entry holes stay open means doing the work a second time.

Things you will not find on this site.

You will not see a founding date, licence number, crew photo, plaque or customer testimonial here. We were not given anything we could verify, and a guess presented as fact would mislead you. To learn about credentials, ask whoever shows up.

Nor do we pledge that rodents will never return. Trees, aged stone walls, estate grounds and a neighbor's crowded garage all keep animals close. What we do provide is a careful inspection, durable materials and a return visit.

Poison, health concerns and other resources.

Federal limits govern the sale and use of rodent poison, and the EPA page linked at the bottom summarizes them. Our preference is traps plus sealing. Should a poison be chosen, you are told which product and exactly where it was placed.

Where droppings must be removed, the CDC order applies: ventilate, soak, wipe, bag. For anything about your own health, see your doctor, as pests are the only matter we can speak on.

Who comes to the property.

A dispatcher arranges the trip, and a technician carries out the work at your address. We print no staff names or pictures, and we assert no qualification that we are unable to document. To know who is coming, simply ask during the call.

Work beyond rodents, such as reroofing, electrical repairs or large drywall jobs, goes to a different trade, and the walk-through points that out.

How pricing and scheduling work.

Pricing follows the walk-through, because a quote given blind is a gamble. The estimate breaks out sealing, cleanup and repair, and you can accept or decline each line.

Same-day arrival depends on that day's bookings. Dispatch reports what is open and promises nothing the calendar cannot support.
