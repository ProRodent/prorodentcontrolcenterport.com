Dealing with droppings without a broom

Use these as a checklist for roughly the first hour after you find droppings.

  • Skip all dry methods. Old pellets turn to powder, and breathing that powder is the very thing to prevent.
  • Trapped air holds whatever has been floating in it, so let a long-sealed space clear out first.
  • Regard every soiled thing as contaminated: gloved while you work, hands scrubbed after.
  • Wet it all, wait briefly, and gather it up with paper towels.
  • Spent towels go into a plastic sack together with any dead rodent, picked up by a gloved hand or tongs, never a bare one.
  • Health questions fall outside our expertise. If you feel unwell following exposure, a clinician is the right person.
