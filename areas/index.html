A caveat belongs at the top. The coverage file has no row for Centerport's own ZIP code, so none is listed for Centerport here. Northport and Huntington carry the closest confirmed codes, about two and a half miles away.

Villages, hamlets and census places.

Long Island is carved into towns, and within those towns are incorporated villages and unincorporated hamlets. The villages here are Northport, Bayville, Farmingdale, Old Westbury, Sea Cliff, Islandia, Lindenhurst and Amityville. Almost every other entry is a hamlet, with no municipal government of its own, a fact that has no bearing on how we trap rodents or seal a home.

Suffolk County supplies the North Shore and south-shore towns, while Nassau County covers the Oyster Bay side and the central plain. Your mailing address might name a village though your lot lies in a hamlet, so we go by ZIP code.

Places we left out.

A handful of nearby communities fell outside our range. The second Hicksville ZIP code sits just beyond fifteen miles, and Port Washington, Uniondale, Great Neck, Centereach, East Meadow, Roslyn and Carle Place also lie outside the circle. If your address straddles a boundary, call and ask.

Questions about how far we travel.

How is distance counted?

Along a straight line joining the centers of two ZIP codes. Actual roads run longer, so dispatch offers a time window over the phone.

What if my ZIP code isn't on the list?

Call regardless and recite your address. A few codes fall slightly beyond the range, and the dispatcher states frankly whether a trip can happen.

Why is there no ZIP code for Centerport on this page?

Our records contain no row for it, and we refuse to make one up. Phone in your street and the address gets confirmed on the spot.

Does a village page include the entire village?

A page shows only the ZIP codes that qualified. A village or hamlet may stretch across more codes than we hold, so the code on your mail is what counts.
