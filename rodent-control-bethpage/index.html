The first frost and what comes after.

Wet weather matters as much as cold. Heavy rain floods burrows and drives animals toward higher, drier ground, which sometimes means a basement window well or a garage corner. A downpour followed by scratching is a pattern worth mentioning.

After the first hard frost, a house that was quiet can suddenly make noise. This is common enough that we ask callers when the sounds started and what the weather did that week.

What first calls sound like.

Calls from Bethpage tend to begin with one of a few observations:

  • Noises in the ceiling after a cold night.
  • A dead rodent found, with worry that others are present.
  • Dark pellets along a baseboard or in a drawer.
  • Droppings in the garage near stored boxes.
