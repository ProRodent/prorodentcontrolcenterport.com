Old Westbury, a village across two towns.

Old Westbury, a Nassau County village incorporated in 1924, has land in the Town of North Hempstead as well as the Town of Oyster Bay. The year is context only. A technician inspects the building in front of them, whatever its age. Pests respond to openings in the structure alone, so give your street for an inspection.

One code, 11568, represents the village in our data. Some lots are large, though we make no assumption about yours, so describe the property and any outbuildings when you call.

Weather and rodent movement.

Winter indoors means closed doors and quiet rooms, which suits animals living in the walls. Sounds are easier to notice at night, and a pantry corner or a heating closet is often the place to listen.

A mild winter does not mean rodents stay away. Warmth near a furnace or water heater can attract them even when the weather outdoors is gentle, so listen for sounds near mechanical rooms.

  • Shredded bedding in a shed or beneath a deck.

Do these things, skip those

The following squeezes down published public health advice, and it applies even if you never book a visit.

  • Vigorous scrubbing kicks up dust, so work gently and keep each surface damp.
  • An attic, garage or shed that has been shut up smells stale for a reason, so open it fully and stay outside for half an hour.
  • Before you handle anything dirty, pull on rubber, vinyl or latex gloves, and scrub your hands carefully at the end.
  • The routine runs spray, rest, wipe, and the rest lasts about five minutes.
  • A dead animal never touches bare skin; bag it and drop the bag in the trash.
  • Linking symptoms to an exposure is a clinician's job, so see one if you become ill.
