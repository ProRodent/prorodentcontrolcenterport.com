Information we gather.

No form, login or comment box exists on this site, so nothing here asks you to type in personal details. When you phone, the dispatcher may note your name, street address, number and a summary of the trouble so a technician can be sent.

Hosting and logs.

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Hosts of this kind commonly record routine request data, such as IP address, browser and the pages fetched. We use it only to keep the site secure and to gauge rough traffic.

Outside content on the pages.

Maps come from Google Maps and fonts from Google Fonts. Each can see your IP address and may place cookies under its own policy. We run no ad trackers and no analytics that follow you from site to site.

What happens to call details.

Details from a call are used to schedule and carry out the job and to check back afterward. We never sell personal information. If another company connects your call, it handles the data under its own terms.

Choices you have.

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction or request deletion, except where a record must be kept by law. Send the request to contact@prorodentcontrolcenterport.com.
