A year in the life of a rodent.

Thaws matter. A warm spell after frozen ground softens soil, and burrowing animals may shift. Fresh mounds near a foundation after a thaw are a classic sign.

After the first hard frost, a house that was quiet can suddenly make noise. This is common enough that we ask callers when the sounds started and what the weather did that week.

Easy steps to take until help arrives

A measured pace keeps fine particles from reaching your lungs.

  • Skip all dry methods. Old pellets turn to powder, and breathing that powder is the very thing to prevent.
  • Trapped air holds whatever has been floating in it, so let a long-sealed space clear out first.
  • Keep gloved fingers away from your face, and wash once the gloves are off.
  • Douse pellets and the patch beneath them in disinfectant or watered-down bleach, then give it roughly five minutes.
  • Pellets, soiled towels and scraps all go in a single bag, tied shut before leaving the house.
  • If something seems off later on, tell a doctor about the cleanup so they can weigh it.
