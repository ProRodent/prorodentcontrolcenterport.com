What happens once it is dark.

Prior to the technician's visit.

  • Move kids and animals to the far end of the house for the moment.
  • Rubber gloves are the minimum before handling anything soiled, wet or chewed.
  • Sticky boards snare birds and chipmunks as readily as mice, so leave them in the store.
  • Take pictures before any tidying, because the mess traces the creatures' routes.

Damp methods only, please

The goal of this list is to keep dust low in the days before somebody visits.

  • Skip all dry methods. Old pellets turn to powder, and breathing that powder is the very thing to prevent.
  • Trapped air holds whatever has been floating in it, so let a long-sealed space clear out first.
  • Gloves should be on from the start, since dirty hands can carry waste to your mouth; soap and water afterward.
  • Wet it all, wait briefly, and gather it up with paper towels.
  • Pick up a dead creature with something between you and it, then place it straight into a bag.
  • Fever, sore muscles or shortness of breath in the weeks after cleaning call for a doctor, and you should say what you handled. A pest firm cannot give medical advice.
