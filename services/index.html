Putting the stages together.

Rarely does a job fit a single page. The common chain is inspect, trap, seal and recheck. Cleanup and repair finish the sequence, because fresh insulation laid down while animals are still inside only gets discarded.

Because every phase is quoted separately, trapping and sealing can be approved now while cleanup or repair waits for a better moment.

Work that belongs to other trades.

There is no separate page for urgent jobs. Urgent callers dial the same number and get the earliest slot. Reroofing, health issues and building repairs unrelated to rodents belong with other professionals, and we say so when they come up.

Questions about the service pages.

May I book only one service?

Yes, any of them stands alone. The technician will point out related work that may be worth adding.

Are raccoons or squirrels part of the work?

The twelve pages deal exclusively with mice and rats. If the inspection turns up another species, we say so and suggest who might help.

Which stage comes first?

Normally the inspection, since it identifies the animal and its entry route. Sealing waits until trapping is complete, so that nothing ends up locked inside a wall.

Do you take on commercial customers?

Yes. Our commercial page describes standing visits that each close with a written report.
