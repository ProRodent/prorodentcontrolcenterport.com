The role of timing on Long Island.

Rodents react to temperature before humans do. Once the nights turn cold, mice and rats look for warmth, and an unsealed seam near the foundation can look inviting. Autumn is the usual start, though thaws and storms can set animals moving again in winter.

Leaf fall hides movement. By late autumn a drift against the siding gives animals cover for the last few feet to a gap, so raking leaves back from the foundation is a sound habit.
