The seasons and your building shell.

Winter indoors means closed doors and quiet rooms, which suits animals living in the walls. Sounds are easier to notice at night, and a pantry corner or a heating closet is often the place to listen.

Wet weather matters as much as cold. Heavy rain floods burrows and drives animals toward higher, drier ground, which sometimes means a basement window well or a garage corner. A downpour followed by scratching is a pattern worth mentioning.

Because rodents entrench fast, an early call tends to be cheaper than the repairs that follow a long wait.
