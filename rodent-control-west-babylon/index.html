Do these things, skip those

Waste from rodents may carry disease, which is why tidying up should be wet, slow and calm. The agencies credited at the foot of the page are the source of each line.

  • Brooms and vacuums are the tools to avoid, because each one hurls settled waste back into the room.
  • After weeks behind a closed door, a room benefits from a through-draft and a patient half hour.
  • Regard every soiled thing as contaminated: gloved while you work, hands scrubbed after.
  • Disinfectant works slowly on waste, so be generous with it and take your time wiping.
  • Pick up a dead creature with something between you and it, then place it straight into a bag.
  • If something seems off later on, tell a doctor about the cleanup so they can weigh it.
