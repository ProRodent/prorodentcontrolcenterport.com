Before we show up.

  • Do not stuff the hole yourself; an animal sealed in will gnaw a second exit beside it.

  • Leave sprays and cleaners in the cabinet until there is a plan.

Do these things, skip those

Use these as a checklist for roughly the first hour after you find droppings.

  • A fast dry sweep looks harmless but lofts particles that hover for ages.
  • CDC guidance calls for roughly thirty minutes of ventilation in a closed space before any cleaning.
  • Before you handle anything dirty, pull on rubber, vinyl or latex gloves, and scrub your hands carefully at the end.
  • Disinfectant works slowly on waste, so be generous with it and take your time wiping.
  • Tie the bag, put it in the bin, then give your hands another wash.
  • Over the next few weeks, keep an eye on how you feel and tell a doctor about flu-like aches or labored breathing.
