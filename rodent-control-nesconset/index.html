The seasons and your building shell.

Thaws matter. A warm spell after frozen ground softens soil, and burrowing animals may shift. Fresh mounds near a foundation after a thaw are a classic sign.

Rodents react to temperature before humans do. Once the nights turn cold, mice and rats look for warmth, and an unsealed seam near the foundation can look inviting. Autumn is the usual start, though thaws and storms can set animals moving again in winter.

Pressing calls and same-day visits.

Weekend calls get the same attention as weekday ones. A dispatcher is on shift, and technicians are assigned according to location and availability.

Winter nights and early mornings are common times for calls. Heat comes on, a hidden animal moves, and a household hears it for the first time.
