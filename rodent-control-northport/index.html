A brief order of operations for cleanup

Pausing before you handle anything protects your lungs at almost no cost.

  • Vigorous scrubbing kicks up dust, so work gently and keep each surface damp.
  • Trapped air holds whatever has been floating in it, so let a long-sealed space clear out first.
  • Gloves should be on from the start, since dirty hands can carry waste to your mouth; soap and water afterward.
  • Wet each pellet and stain thoroughly, then allow the liquid to do its work.
  • The soiled towels may go in with the dead animal inside one tied bag, and a second bag goes over anything thin.
  • Over the next few weeks, keep an eye on how you feel and tell a doctor about flu-like aches or labored breathing.
