Health agency advice, boiled down

Pausing before you handle anything protects your lungs at almost no cost.

  • Skip all dry methods. Old pellets turn to powder, and breathing that powder is the very thing to prevent.
  • After weeks behind a closed door, a room benefits from a through-draft and a patient half hour.
  • Shield your hands during the task, then lather up under running water at the end.
  • Coat the waste and the floor around it, give it a rest, and only then make the first pass with a towel.
  • The soiled towels may go in with the dead animal inside one tied bag, and a second bag goes over anything thin.
  • Over the next few weeks, keep an eye on how you feel and tell a doctor about flu-like aches or labored breathing.
