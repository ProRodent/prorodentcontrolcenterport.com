Do these things, skip those

These are plain-language summaries of federal health pages, not medical counsel. Take personal health questions to your own doctor.

  • Wiping with a dry rag raises the same particles that health agencies caution about.
  • CDC guidance calls for roughly thirty minutes of ventilation in a closed space before any cleaning.
  • Shield your hands during the task, then lather up under running water at the end.
  • Saturate before you collect, because the pause holds dust down during the wipe.
  • Spent towels go into a plastic sack together with any dead rodent, picked up by a gloved hand or tongs, never a bare one.
  • Feeling sick after contact with droppings is for a physician to judge, so tell them what you touched.
